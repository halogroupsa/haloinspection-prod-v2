HALO PROPERTY INSPECTION — PREPARING FOR YOUR VISIT

1. Confirm the agreed inspection scope, fee, date and report turnaround.
2. Arrange permission and access with the owner, developer or building manager.
3. Keep electricity and water connected where possible.
4. Make rooms, bathrooms, balconies and agreed service areas accessible.
5. Tell Halo about disconnected utilities, inaccessible areas or safety concerns.
6. Share your handover notice, previous snag list or relevant renovation scope.
7. Note any recurring leaks, AC issues or other concerns for the inspector.
8. Keep the report for your discussions with the developer, seller or contractor.
9. Ask about a secondary inspection after rectification, if needed.
